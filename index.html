Google Gemini API Key

Gemini is Google's free AI — it reads business cards accurately. The free tier gives you 15 scans per minute and requires no credit card.

📋 How to get your FREE Gemini API key (2 minutes): 1. Go to aistudio.google.com/app/apikey
2. Sign in with any Google account (Gmail)
3. Click "Create API key"
4. Copy the key and paste it above
5. Tap Save — you're done!

✅ Free tier: 15 requests/min · 1,500 requests/day · No credit card · No expiry
Privacy
• Your API key is stored only on this device in localStorage
• Card images are sent to Google Gemini only for OCR extraction
• No data is stored on any server — only your device
• Contacts are saved locally in your browser only
• You can clear all data anytime using the button below
Install as iPhone App
1. Open this page in Safari
2. Tap the Share button (box with arrow)
3. Tap "Add to Home Screen"
4. Tap Add — done!

The app will work offline after first load (contacts, editing, export — all offline). Only scanning needs internet.
